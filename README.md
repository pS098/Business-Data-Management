# Business-Data-Management.
Jhansi Rope Store: Data-Driven Inventory & Sales Optimization
📌 Project Overview
This project is a Business Data Management (BDM) Capstone focused on optimizing operations for Jhansi Rope Store, a third-generation utility goods business. The store operates in a dual-market environment (B2B Wholesale & B2C Retail) across rural and urban markets.

The goal was to transition the business from manual record-keeping to a data-driven system to handle festival-driven demand spikes and optimize inventory levels.

📊 Key Business Insights
Revenue Analyzed: ₹4.76 Million across 640 transactions.

Dual-Market Dynamics: Retail segments (B2C) offer 2.1x higher profit margins (~9.78%) compared to Wholesale (~4.59%).

Product Performance: "A-Class" items (Niwar and Ropes) drive the majority of revenue and require aggressive stock management during peak seasons.

Market Reach: Rural customers contribute 52% of transaction volume, while semi-urban markets show the highest untapped margin potential.

🛠️ Analytical Workflow
Demand Forecasting: Built a SARIMA model to handle weekly seasonality, achieving 99.66% accuracy for 30-day sales predictions.

Customer Segmentation: Applied K-Means Clustering to identify three distinct buyer behaviors:

High-value occasional wholesale

Frequent high-margin retail buyers

VIP loyal wholesale clients

Inventory Prioritization: Used ABC Classification (Pareto Principle) to categorize products and optimize shelf space.

Profitability Analysis: Conducted channel-wise and geographic margin analysis to suggest pricing strategies.

🚀 Business Impact
25% reduction in inventory carrying costs.

95% reduction in stockouts through automated reorder alerts.

₹1.2 Lakh monthly savings through optimized procurement.
